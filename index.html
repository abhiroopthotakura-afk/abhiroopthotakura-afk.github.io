About

From the PLC rung to the production floor.

I'm an Automation & Controls Engineer at General Motors in Dayton, Ohio, where I support 24x7 manufacturing by diagnosing PLC and control-system faults and getting production-critical equipment back online.

My work spans Allen-Bradley ControlLogix and CompactLogix, Omron NJ/NX/CJ and Siemens PLCs, FactoryTalk View and Ignition HMI/SCADA, servo drives and VFDs, FANUC robot/PMC integration and Cognex vision systems.

Before GM, I built PLC/HMI programs and ran FDA IQ/OQ/PQ validation for medical device lines, and automated diesel engine assembly lines and test cells. An M.S. in Business Intelligence & Analytics lets me turn PLC alarm and cycle data into uptime and quality improvements.
